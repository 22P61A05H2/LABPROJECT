## Algoveda Event Report

Vignana Bharathi Institution of Technology successfully hosted Algoveda, a coding-focused event, on its campus located at Office road, Ghatkesar mandal, Medchal–Malkajgiri, Telangana, 500088, India. The event fostered a vibrant and engaging atmosphere for participants, visibly themed around algorithmic problem-solving. 

Algoveda saw enthusiastic participation from students keen on enhancing their coding skills. Feedback indicated a positive experience, with attendees specifically noting the event’s value for coding enthusiasts and the opportunity for collaborative learning with peers. The institution aims to build upon this success with future technical initiatives.
