## Algoveda Event Report

Vignana Bharathi Institution of Technology successfully hosted “Algoveda,” a focused event centered around algorithmic understanding. The event fostered a vibrant atmosphere of technical engagement amongst participants at the VBIT campus located in Ghatkesar mandal, Medchal–Malkajgiri, Telangana. Visual branding prominently featured “AlgoVeda” and “VBIT” throughout the venue. 

Participant engagement was notable, demonstrating strong student interest in the subject matter. Feedback indicated attendees valued the event's content but highlighted challenges relating to network connectivity and a perceived need for more readily available event coordination. The registration fee of Rs. 199/- was also noted as potentially prohibitive by some respondents. Future iterations will address these concerns.
